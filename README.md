# Meridian Shell

A customizable, themeable replacement for the Windows 11 taskbar, Start menu and File Explorer, in one look you choose.

## ⬇️ [Download Meridian Shell 1.0 beta 1](https://github.com/meridiandrive/Meridian-Shell/releases/download/v1.0-beta-1/MeridianShell-Setup-1.0-beta-1.exe)

Windows 11, 64-bit · installs for your account only, no administrator rights needed · [all versions](https://github.com/meridiandrive/Meridian-Shell/releases)

> **This is a beta.** Expect rough edges. **Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Q** always closes Meridian's bar and gives you the Windows taskbar back.

---

## What you get

- **The bar:** one bar, or separate modules (Start, your apps, the notification area) on any edge of any screen. Docked on the left or right, they stand upright.
- **Start:** your pinned programs, search, recent files and power options, plus a small Start at the pointer when you middle-click the desktop.
- **File Manager:** tabs, previews, folder sizes and Windows' own right-click menus, in the same look as the bar.
- **Meridian Settings:** themes, colours, glass and blur, fonts, and effects, plus Windows' everyday settings in one place.
- **Desktop widgets:** CPU, memory, GPU and network cards on your wallpaper.
- **A real console** on the bar: Cmd and PowerShell, always ready.
- **Work areas:** virtual desktops with numbered buttons on the bar.

Nothing of Windows is removed. Meridian runs beside it, and you can go back at any time.

## Installing

1. Download the installer above and run it.
2. Windows may say *"Windows protected your PC"*, because the installer isn't signed yet. Choose **More info > Run anyway**.
3. A short intro shows on the first start. You can open it again from Meridian Settings > About.

Installing a newer version keeps your settings.

## If something goes wrong

- **Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Q** closes Meridian's bar and brings the Windows taskbar back.
- Right-click the bar > **Exit to Explorer**.
- `RECOVERY.txt` in Meridian's folder explains every way back.

## Uninstalling

Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > **Meridian Shell** > Uninstall. Windows' own taskbar comes back as it was.
